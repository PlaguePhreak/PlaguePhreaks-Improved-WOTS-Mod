v0.28.18 hotfix - one change in ModAAEvade.Apply (Edit Method), after you have added ModShipHelm
===============================================================================================
1. Add Class (C#), delete everything, paste the whole of 5_NEW_ModShipHelm.cs.txt, then Compile.
2. Open ModAAEvade, right-click Apply, and choose Edit Method.
3. The first "if" in the method ends with:

		|| ModPlayerOverride.OnHelm(parentUnit))

4. Change it to:

		|| ModPlayerOverride.OnHelm(parentUnit) || ModShipHelm.Skip(ai, parentUnit))

   (dnSpy may call "parentUnit" something else, for example "unit". Use the same name that is inside OnHelm( ). "ai" is the method's own
   parameter, so look at the top line, public static void Apply(UnitAI ai), and use that name.)
5. Compile.

Knob at the top of ModShipHelm: SkipManualShips = false lets your hand-steered ships (not the selected one) dodge again.
