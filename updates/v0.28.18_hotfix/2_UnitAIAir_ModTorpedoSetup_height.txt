v0.28.18 hotfix - torpedo planes stay high until the split (fixes "fighters won't engage torpedo bombers")
======================================================================================================
Cause (from the tester's output_log): the torpedo planes dropped to their 40 m attack height as soon as the
attack started, then flew the whole new run-up and gather (several minutes) at 40 m. Fighters never go below
200 m (ModDogfight.MinHeight), so the Yaks made 262 passes on the Kates but only fired 36 times.

Now the Kates fly the run-up and gather at 800 m, and only go down to attack height after the split.

Steps (dnSpy):
1. Open UnitAIAir, right-click ModTorpedoSetup, Edit Method.
2. Find the block you added in step 11 (it starts with  Vector3 modAhead; ).
3. In it, find this line:

		this.parentUnit.wantedHeight = this.SetAircraftWantedHeight(this.attackHeight);

4. Change it to:

		this.parentUnit.wantedHeight = this.SetAircraftWantedHeight(Mathf.Max(this.attackHeight, 80f));

5. Compile, then File > Save Module.

(80 = 800 m. A bigger number keeps them higher; do not go under 30, or fighters lose them again.)
