Three problems from Aaron's message are confirmed in the log or the code: the chaser cannot close on an equally fast target (log), friends break away from each other in pairs and a formation member leaves its slot to avoid a flight mate (log), and the player's sliders lose against the AI (code, not the log).

## 4. Why

* **Chase.** `GunRun` already asks for 1.1 x top speed (15.6), but the thrust model holds the plane at its top speed (14.2), so a target at 13.9 to 14.2 cannot be caught by wanted speed alone. Chase power gives a chaser more thrust than the target can have: +35% thrust (about +10% top speed), 240 s per sortie, rested at 0.15 s per second, 1.8 times the fuel burn, only while the target is farther than 300 m and the chaser is less than 12% faster. 14.2 becomes about 15.6, a closing speed of about 15 m/s, so 3 km takes about 200 s.
* **Friends.** `ModAirCollide.Avoid` made both planes of a pair break (turn at 90% of the limit, 25 m height change, 0.9 s) and only skipped slow cruising pairs (closing under 25 m/s). Flight mates in `Follow_Unit` now yield (`ModAirFormation.Yield`: slot shifted 50 m sideways and 20 m up or down for 2.5 s, then back), the leader holds course. Any other friendly pair: only the plane with the higher id gives way. A real collision course (paths inside the two radii within 1 s) still breaks both.
* **Controls.** `CombatInterface.TurnSlider/HeightSlider/SpeedSlider` put the old slider value back while a unit is in `Follow_Unit` or `Follow_Nav_Points` or a manoeuvre (so a route or formation cannot be steered), and an `Attack_Unit` order is re-steered by the AI every tick. For planes `ModFlight.Begin` runs `ModAirCollide.Avoid` for every plane, including the one the player flies. For ships `ModAAEvade.Apply` (mod class) takes the rudder and throttle for 20 to 40 s whenever planes attack and then restores the old values; the stock hazard evasion already skips the selected ship. New class `ModPlayerOverride` is called as the first line of the three sliders: for a player unit it clears the route, formation and attack (action to Nothing), ends a break and cancels an AA evasion; `OnHelm` stops the AA evasion for 30 s; `IsManual` stops the avoidance steering a plane in your hand. Selecting a unit, starting a battle and a unit switch are ignored for 1 s so the game's own slider refresh does not count as the player.

Nothing in this was run in the game. It was compiled against the DLL from the 19th, one class at a time in the install order.
