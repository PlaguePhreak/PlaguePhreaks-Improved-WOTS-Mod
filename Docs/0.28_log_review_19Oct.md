A loss for the Wildcats (17 of 23 down against 6 of about 27), and partly an avoidable one: 8 of the 23 total losses are collisions, and 4 of the last 5 Wildcat losses happened during my new Recover move. Technically the update ran: no crash, all new systems logged.

## 3. Tweaks Needed? (most important first)
1. **Collisions are too lethal and friendly head-ons should not happen.** Change: head-on (both lost) only for hostile pairs or at a much higher closing speed; friendlies avoid with 100% sight. Also count "head-on" by the angle between headings, not closing speed alone.
2. **Recover move leaves the Wildcat defenceless with a Zero on its tail.** Change: cancel Recover (go to Defend) when an enemy is within about 400 m behind.
3. **Wildcats keep attacking with a Zero on their tail.** Change: 7 of the 13 gun/fire losses were in phase 0 (attack) with "last enemy on its tail: A6M5". Defend trigger needs to fire sooner.
4. **Pilot ranks look lopsided.** Check the rank mix: Zeros 74% Veteran/Elite, Wildcats 39% (15% Green). That alone may explain 175 hits against 23. Check whether this is intended (campaign setting) before touching the aim code.
5. **Parts naming.** Wing loss on the Wildcat detaches `RearFlap` and `L_WingAttach`, tail loss detaches `L_Rear_Fin`. Better names exist (`L_WingMain`, `L_Wing_FlapOuter`, `L_RearElevator`). The Zero (`A6M5_Mod52C`) has one mesh: no visual parts possible, effect only.
6. **Low:** bomb drops are logged as [BOMBAIM] "high level release ... on Wildcat" (log wording only); destroyed planes keep printing [FLIGHT] lines (18,104 lines total); 2 x `IndexOutOfRangeException` in `WeaponMountLinked.UpdateMounts` (lines 73269 and 83535), cause unknown.

## 4. Why
1. Collisions: 8 collision losses (4 Wildcats, 4 Zeros). Wildcat-Wildcat head-on at 167 m/s (381 s) and Zero-Zero head-on at 98 m/s (478 s) took out 4 own planes. Of 6 Zero losses, 4 were collisions, so the Wildcats killed only 2 Zeros with guns (one shot down at 419 s, one fuel fire at 495 s). Collisions cost both sides, so they hide the true gun result.
2. Recover: losses at 421, 503, 511, 518 s are phase 6 (moves) with a Zero on the tail. A plane that is extending and climbing slowly is an easy target.
3. Attack phase: losses at 340, 349, 365, 365, 441, 441, 450 s are phase 0 with a Zero on the tail.
4. Hit rate: ours 23 hits from 77 bursts (0.3 per burst) against 175 from 172 (1.0 per burst). Energy was equal, so energy is not the cause. Rank mix is the visible difference.
