# Second test log review (output_log.txt, 94,633 lines, ends at about 409 s)

## 0. The build in this log does not match the 0.28.1 files
- [PARTS] still throws off `RearFlap` and `L_WingAttach` as "wing" (5 + 2 times, 1 x `L_WingMain`). The 0.28.1 code cannot pick `RearFlap`.
- Destroyed planes are logged every second (3,933 of 20,290 [FLIGHT] lines are Shot_Down), and every plane is logged every 1 s. 0.28 / 0.28.1 `ModFlightLog` logs every 10 s.
- The [BATTLE] line ends after the energy part. The 0.28 version adds "; collisions ..." and the move counts.
- No [FLIGHT] line has an engine, wing or tail percentage, a LEAK or a FIRE tag, although 291 hits landed and 7 wings were shed. No Immelmann, pitchback, scissors, decoy or recover lines either.
- But collisions behave like 0.28.1 (friendly "bad collision" at 106 to 176 m/s, not a head-on crash).
So some of the 10 classes in the game are older than the files. Conclusions below about the new fixes are therefore not safe.

## 1. What happened
- Setup: 24 Wildcats against 36 A7M2 Sam (Japanese 1945 fighter). Zero was the enemy in the last test, so this is a different and much harder match-up. Sustained turn 13.5 deg/s (Wildcat) against 17.0 (Sam).
- 99 s: still approaching. 116 and 117 s: a Sam-Sam and a Wildcat-Wildcat collision, one lost on each side.
- 159 s: kills 2 to 2, but hits on aircraft 13 for us and 42 against.
- 219 s: Wildcats lost 6, Sams lost 2. 279 s: 10 against 4. 339 s: 17 against 7. 400 s: 21 against 8.
- The log ends at about 409 s with 3 Wildcats against 28 Sams. Wildcats destroyed: 21 of 24. Sams destroyed: 8 of 36, of which 5 were friendly collisions (all 6 collisions in the log were friendly: 5 Sam-Sam, 1 Wildcat-Wildcat). The Wildcats shot down 3 Sams (149, 231, 262 s).
- Hits on aircraft at the end: 37 ours, 291 theirs. Bursts: 91 against 265. The fight stayed between 4.0 and 5.2 km, energy equal (last 60 s at 400 s: 4,599 m against 4,665 m).

## 2. Verdict
A heavy, mostly unavoidable loss: 24 Wildcats against 36 better fighters. The numbers say the match-up decided it, but the Wildcats also threw away planes: 6 died in the "extend away" phase with a Sam behind them and 10 were still in the attack phase when shot.

## 3. Tweaks needed? (most important first)
1. **Confirm which classes are really installed** (see section 0) before judging 0.28.1. In dnSpy, search each class for: `NameScore` (ModAirDamage), `TailCloseRange` (ModDogfight), `wrecks` (ModFlightLog). If one is missing, do Edit Class on that class alone with its own file from `2_replace_existing_classes` or `1_add_new_classes`.
2. If those are all present and it still looks like this: the defence trigger needs another look (4 Wildcats died in phase 4, the defend phase itself).
3. Match-up: do not judge Wildcat tactics against Sams at 24 against 36. Use the 1v1 and 4v4 tests first.
4. Friendly collisions still cost 5 Sams and 1 Wildcat. Make friendly "bad collision" non-lethal (damage only) and widen the friendly avoid margin.

## 4. Why
1. The marker list in section 0 is evidence for a mixed build, not for a tactics problem.
2. Phases of Wildcat losses (excluding the collision): phase 0 about 10, phase 6 six, phase 4 four. Examples: id -51912 defended at 296 s ("breaking and diving away"), switched to "extending away" at 308 s and died at 314 s. Same for -50042 (306 s defend, 317 s extend, 322 s dead).
3. Hits per burst: 0.41 for the Wildcats and 1.1 for the Sams. With a worse turn rate (13.5 against 17.0) the Wildcats rarely get a firing solution.
4. All 6 collisions were between friendly planes (closing 58 to 176 m/s). 0 collisions between enemies. Collisions cost the Sams 5 planes, so they flatter the result for the Wildcats.
