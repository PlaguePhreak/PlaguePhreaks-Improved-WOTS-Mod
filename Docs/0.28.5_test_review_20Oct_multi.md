## 2. Verdict
Three of the four small and medium battles were poor performances for the Wildcat side (0 of 4 Zeros killed in battle 3, 1 of 4 in battle 4, 2 of 12 in battle 5 against 4, 8 and 7 lost). The last battle was a messy win. The new features work in the log (player override, fuel ditching, chase power on), but friend-on-friend collisions are costing planes and the chase boost did not help the one chase that was tested.

## 3. Tweaks Needed
1. Friend collisions: 5 of 7 collisions were friend against friend, 4 closing at 150 to 184 m/s, and 5 planes were lost to them (about a third of the Wildcats lost in battle 5). Needs a stronger, earlier break at those speeds, or a vertical separation rule for friends.
2. The chaser in battle 2 never used its speed: it was climbing and diving at 60% of top speed while the target ran. When the target is flying a route and not fighting back, the chaser should skip the energy game and run it down in level flight.
3. Formation yield never ran in this test (0 yields). Not proven to work, not proven broken; needs a test where a flight is in formation for longer (the combat spread starts within seconds in every battle).
4. The Wildcat tactics against the Zero: 35 + 10 "energy fight: extending away" and 13 "Zero on her tail, breaking and diving away" decisions in battle 3 against a Zero turning 19.0 against 13.5 deg/s; 4 of 4 losses had a Zero on the tail. Candidate: mutual cover (the 0.26 team moves exist: sandwich, decoy) used more, or a bigger flight turning into the attacker instead of extending alone.
5. Still open: Immelmann never started; scissors worked 0 of 5; the [AVOID] log stops after 60 lines so the later collisions have no avoid evidence.

## 4. Why
* Collisions: [COLLIDE] lines: Zero-Zero 2089 m (156 m/s), Wildcat-Wildcat 1273 s (150 m/s, both lost), Zero-Zero 1344 s (159), Zero-Zero 1679 s (184), Wildcat-Wildcat 2037 s (44 m/s, one lost), plus two head-on crashes with the enemy (168, 221 m/s). The avoidance looks 2 s ahead and breaks for 0.9 s; at 150 m/s a pair that closes in a turning fight has little room. Right of way worked (single [AVOID] lines instead of pairs: 10 of the first 60 lines were friends, mostly one plane at a time, against 14 of 60 in pairs before).
* Chase: the boost counter (828) counts each time the request switches on, so it is flicker; the log cannot show a gain. In battle 2 the Wildcat's speed (8.2 to 10.7) is far below the Zero's 14.5, so extra thrust at the top of the speed range never came into play.
* Formation: in this log every flight with `Follow_Unit` at spawn was in `Attack_Unit` by the time a friend pair needed to avoid (the pairs I checked at 1246 s), so the yield branch had no case.
* Player override: [PLAYER] at 86 s and 665 s; the plane that was `Attack_Unit` at 80 s was `Nothing` at 90 s and kept height 3,330 and turn 0 until the log moved on. Shows the first part works; whether the ship part and the hand-flown avoidance work was not tested in this log.
