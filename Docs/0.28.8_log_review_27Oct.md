## Findings (all fixed in v0.28.9_update)
1. **Burst cancelling (bug).** GunRun judges a burst by burstStart[id]. The fire key, Defend's wingman cover and RollAttack switched the guns on without setting it, so the burst was stopped on the next tick as older than MaxBurst, and burstRest blocked the AI's fire for 2 s. Log: Wildcat -15224, 854 to 858 s, "burst ended after 192.5 s ... 196.4 s" eight times at 337 to 269 m, 1.2 to 1.9 deg off; another at 1703 m, 158 deg off (fire key pressed with no target ahead).

3. **Threat range 250 m < Zero gun range 300 m.** Zeros open fire at 268 to 300 m; the Wildcat only notices a plane on its tail inside 250 m. All 19 "on her tail" lines are at 184 to 249 m. Wildcat -15988 died in phase 0 (attack) with a Zero on its tail.
4. **Slow worse turner turns with flaps.** Decide picks ModeTurn when it cannot run (top speed 14.2 vs 14.5, below 2500 m); flaps out inside 1000 m bleed speed; Wildcats stuck below corner speed. Now flaps in for the worse turner except a snap shot.
5. **Acrobatics with a threat behind.** Wildcat -16362 destroyed in phase 5 (barrel roll attack) with a Zero on its tail. Barrel roll attack and Immelmann now need s.threat == null.
6. Cosmetic: "[BATTLE] ... collisions collisions 0".
