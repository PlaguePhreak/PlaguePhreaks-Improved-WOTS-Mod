
<b><size=18>OPERATION WATCHTOWER</size></b>

<b>August 7th 1942</b>
Japanese forces have landed on <color=#caa809>GUADALCANAL</color> and begun construction of an airfield. Once finished, long range bombers will be free to attack the supply lines between America and Australia.

Using <color=#caa809>NEW HEBRIDES</color> and <color=#caa809>COOKTOWN</color> as home ports, your orders are to expel Japan from the South Pacific, crush all resistance and take her home port at <color=#caa809>RABAUL</color>.


You must:

1) Take control of the enemy home port:
   - <color=#caa809>RABAUL</color>

2) Take control of <color=#caa809>GUADALCANAL</color> and build a rank 5 airfield there

3) Secondary objectives:
   - Take <color=#caa809>LAE</color> to use as a forward operating base to strike at <color=#caa809>RABAUL</color>

4) Maintain control of:
   - <color=#caa809>NEW HEBRIDES</color>
   - <color=#caa809>COOKTOWN</color>
   - <color=#caa809>RENNELL ISLAND</color>

All of these must be held at the same time to win the campaign.
