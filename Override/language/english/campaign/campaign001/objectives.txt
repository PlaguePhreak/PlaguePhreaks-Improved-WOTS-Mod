<b>COUNTERATTACK ON THE AUSTRALIAN FRONT</b>

August 7th 1942
The Japanese advance has been halted at the gates of Australia, but their forces still hold New Guinea and the Solomon Islands. Their main base, <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color>, feeds every attack against our supply lines between America and Australia.
The Marines are landing on <color=#E0B030>GUADALCANAL</color> and the <color=#E0B030>FLORIDA ISLANDS</color> today. The time to strike back is now.

Your orders are to expel the Japanese from the South Pacific and crush all resistance, by taking their home port.


You must:

1) Capture <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color>, the Japanese home port

2) Maintain control of:
   - <color=#E0B030>COOKTOWN</color>
   - <color=#E0B030>NEW HEBRIDES</color>

The campaign is lost if the Japanese hold <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color>, <color=#E0B030>COOKTOWN</color> and <color=#E0B030>NEW HEBRIDES</color> at the same time.
