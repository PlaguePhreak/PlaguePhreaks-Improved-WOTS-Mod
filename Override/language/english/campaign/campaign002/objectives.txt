
<b><size=18>OPERATION SN: GUADALCANAL</size></b>

<b>August 7th 1942</b>
A small landing force of our troops has already occupied <color=#caa809>GUADALCANAL</color>. The Allied counteroffensive is expected soon, and the Empire must hold its southern perimeter while pushing the enemy back.

Your orders are to take <color=#caa809>COOKTOWN</color> to build a foothold on Australia, take <color=#caa809>NEW HEBRIDES</color> to cut the Allied supply line, and keep <color=#caa809>RABAUL</color>, our home port, safe.


You must:

1) Take control of the enemy home ports:
   - <color=#caa809>COOKTOWN</color>
   - <color=#caa809>NEW HEBRIDES</color>

2) Build a rank 5 airfield on <color=#caa809>GUADALCANAL</color>

3) Secondary objectives:
   - Take <color=#caa809>RENNELL ISLAND</color> to use as a forward operating base to strike at <color=#caa809>NEW HEBRIDES</color>

4) Maintain control of:
   - <color=#caa809>RABAUL</color>
   - <color=#caa809>LAE</color>
   - <color=#caa809>GUADALCANAL</color>

All of these must be held at the same time to win the campaign.
