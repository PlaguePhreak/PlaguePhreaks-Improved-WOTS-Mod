<b>DEFENDING JAPAN'S SOUTHWEST PACIFIC INTERESTS</b>

August 7th 1942
A small landing force of our troops has already occupied <color=#E0B030>GUADALCANAL</color> and the <color=#E0B030>FLORIDA ISLANDS</color>. <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color> is the hub of our advance, and the American counteroffensive is coming.
Hold what we have taken, push back the American offensive and take more locations as needed. Once the foothold in the south is secure, long range bombers will be free to attack Allied supply lines between America and Australia.

Your orders are to take <color=#E0B030>COOKTOWN</color> and <color=#E0B030>NEW HEBRIDES</color> and break the Allied hold on the South Pacific.


You must:

1) Capture <color=#E0B030>COOKTOWN</color>, to build a foothold on Australia

2) Capture <color=#E0B030>NEW HEBRIDES</color>

3) Maintain control of <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color>, our home port

The campaign is lost if the Allies take <color=#E0B030>RABAUL</color> while holding <color=#E0B030>COOKTOWN</color> and <color=#E0B030>NEW HEBRIDES</color>.
